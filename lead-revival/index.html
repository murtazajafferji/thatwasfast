Questions people always ask.

Isn't this just spam?
No — and the difference is legal, not just cosmetic. These are people who contacted you first: asked for a quote, booked a job, filled out your form. That's an existing business relationship. We scrub anyone who ever opted out, honor quiet hours, put opt-out language in the first message, and register the campaign properly. We won't text a purchased or scraped list, and we'll turn down the job if that's what you have.
How big does my list need to be?
Around 1,000 contacts is where the math starts working. Below that, a campaign can still pay for itself if your average job is large, but we'd rather tell you it's thin than take your money. Bring your number to the consult and we'll do the arithmetic with you on the call.
What if my list is old?
Old is fine — often better. A two-year-old quote is someone whose situation has almost certainly changed. Past customers from three years ago are frequently the strongest segment, because they already paid you once and liked it. We adjust the opener to acknowledge the gap rather than pretend it isn't there.
Will this annoy my past customers?
It's a short, polite, personal-sounding text that's easy to ignore or opt out of — not a marketing blast. Every script gets your approval before it sends. In practice the most common reply is some version of "oh good, I've been meaning to call you."
What happens when it works and my phone blows up?
That's the good problem, and it's worth planning for. The AI books straight into your calendar, so most of the volume never needs a human. If you want the overflow answered too, our voice receptionist handles the calls and lead response catches new inbound — and your $1,997 comes straight off the price.
Can you guarantee results?
We won't promise you a number, because your list, your industry, and your reputation drive the outcome more than we do. What we will do is tell you honestly on the consult whether we think it's worth running, show you the math, and give you every real number at the end — good or bad. If the honest answer is "your list is too small," you'll hear that instead of an invoice.
Do I need to be on a monthly plan?
No. This is a standalone one-time campaign with no contract and nothing recurring. Most people run it precisely because they're not ready to commit to a system yet — it's the cheapest way to find out whether AI actually moves the needle in your business. If it does, the full $1,997 credits toward whatever you build next.
