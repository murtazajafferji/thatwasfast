Money questions,
answered straight.

If it's not here, ask on the consult. We don't do surprise line items.

Is the $597/mo per system?

No, and this is the part people usually get wrong in our favor. Setup fees are per system built. The monthly is one flat fee covering unlimited support, tuning, and adjustments across everything we've built for you. One system: $597/mo. Four systems: still $597/mo. Come back next quarter for a fifth: still $597/mo.

What am I actually paying the monthly for?

Hosting and monitoring, unlimited support calls, tuning from real call and lead data, updates when models improve, and a monthly check-in on performance. It's not a license fee for software sitting still — it's the part that keeps the system getting better as your business changes.

Are there usage costs on top?

Your system runs on your own accounts (OpenAI, Vapi, etc.), so you pay those providers directly at cost — typically $30–$150/mo depending on call volume. We don't mark it up or resell minutes, which is exactly how we avoid the per-minute overage anxiety you get with Smith.ai or Ruby.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Three-month minimum on the monthly, then cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. The systems we built stay yours — they run on your accounts, on your phone number, with your data. You're not renting access to something we could switch off.

What does the 30-day guarantee actually cover?

The setup fee. If you're not seeing measurable results in the first 30 days after go-live, we refund it. No hoops, no fine print, no "prove you followed the process" games. We'd rather return money than have someone tell twenty other business owners it didn't work.

Why is the DIY Blueprint so cheap compared to the build?

Because you're doing the work. The knowledge is the same, the labor isn't. If you get partway through and decide it's not the best use of your time, the entire $497 is credited toward a Done-For-You build — so you've lost nothing but a weekend, and you'll understand your own system better than most of our clients do.

What if my business is too small?

Then the DIY Blueprint or a single voice receptionist is where you start. We'd rather build you one thing that clearly pays for itself and earn the second one than sell you a stack you can't absorb. If we don't think it'll work for you yet, we'll tell you on the consult — that call has ended with "not yet, call us in six months" more than once.

Do the referral discounts and partner commissions stack?

Referral discounts stack with each other down to $0/mo. Affiliate commissions are for people sending clients regularly rather than occasionally — if you're doing both, we'll put you on whichever pays you more, not whichever pays us more.
