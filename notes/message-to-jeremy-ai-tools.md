# Message to Jeremy — which AI tool to use

**Short version: get Claude Pro at $20/month and use Cowork. Don't start with Perplexity.**

They're not really competitors — they do different jobs. Here's the plain-English version.

---

## Claude Cowork — "an assistant that does the work"

You give it a goal, it goes and does the multi-step task: opens files on your computer, edits them, organizes folders, drafts documents, works through a checklist. You watch it work and step in when needed.

It's built specifically for non-coders — that was the whole point of it. If you can describe the task to a new hire, you can hand it to Cowork.

**What it's actually good at for you:**
- Take messy call notes → turn into a clean client summary + follow-up email
- Build an onboarding packet for a new client from our template
- Go through a folder of documents and pull out the numbers you need
- Prep your monthly check-in docs — pull the client's numbers, draft the agenda
- Keep the referral tracker current instead of you rebuilding it each month

**Cost:** included in Claude **Pro, $20/month** ($17 if you pay yearly). Works on Mac, Windows, web, and phone. You do *not* need the $100 or $200 plan to start.

**The honest catch:** it's still labeled a "research preview." It's genuinely useful but it's not flawless — it runs slow sometimes, and you need to check its work. Treat it like a sharp intern, not an autopilot.

---

## Perplexity — "an assistant that finds things out"

Perplexity is a research tool at heart. Best-in-class for *"what's true, and what's the source?"*

They do have agent-style products:
- **Comet** — their AI browser. Free. It can read the page you're on and click through things for you: fill a form, pull info off a site, compare options across tabs.
- **Labs** — builds a thing for you from a prompt: a report, a dashboard, a simple mini web app.
- **Perplexity Max** ($200/month) unlocks their heavier agentic mode.

**What it's actually good at for you:**
- Researching a prospect before a call — their business, size, reviews, competitors
- Competitive checks: what are other shops in this space charging and claiming?
- Pulling market stats with real citations you can quote
- Anything where the browser is the workplace

**The honest catch:** independent testing puts the agentic browsing at roughly **80% success on multi-step tasks** — so it fails about one try in five. Fine for research, frustrating if you're depending on it to finish a job.

---

## The actual difference

**Cowork works on your stuff. Perplexity works on the internet.**

Your day is documents, clients, follow-ups, and internal process — that's Cowork's territory. Perplexity is the better research tool, but research isn't where your hours are going.

There is no Perplexity product that's a true Cowork equivalent. Comet is the closest, and it's a different category: it drives a browser, not your workflow.

---

## What I'd do

1. **Start with Claude Pro, $20/month.** One tool, one bill, lowest friction.
2. **Pick one repetitive task you do every week** — I'd suggest the post-call summary and follow-up email — and get Cowork doing just that until it's reliable.
3. **Then add one more.** Don't try to automate five things at once; you'll get frustrated and quit.
4. **Add Perplexity later, free, only if you find yourself doing a lot of prospect research.** The free Comet browser covers it. Don't pay $200/month for Max — that's a power-user tier and you won't need it.

Skip the "which AI is best" rabbit hole. $20 and one workflow beats a perfect tool you never set up.
