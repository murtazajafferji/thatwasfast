## Part 2 — Answer these

None of this is in your inbox. One line each, and don't polish it.

**The inventory — this is the part I most need**

1. **List every booth setup you own.** For each one: what you call it, what it is (open-air, enclosed, mirror, 360, roaming/iPad, print vs digital), and **how many of that exact setup you have.** If you own two identical open-airs and one 360, say that — the count is the whole point.
2. What do you charge for each, and what's included at each level — hours, prints, props, backdrop, attendant, digital gallery, custom branding?
3. Which one gets booked most? Which one do you wish sold more?
4. Can two of your setups run at two different events on the same day right now, today, with no new purchases?
5. What's missing to run a third simultaneous event — another booth, a printer, a vehicle, or just a person?
6. What did each setup cost, and is anything financed or still being paid off?
7. Is anything sitting unused because it's a hassle, needs repair, or never sells?

**Staffing**

8. Do you have someone in mind at $20/hr, or is that a number you've priced but not hired?
9. What does training someone actually take — an afternoon, a shadowed event, more?
10. What can only you do: the pitch, the setup, the editing afterward, dealing with the venue?

**The economics**

11. Beyond the $20/hr, what does one event cost you — travel, prints, consumables, replacement parts?
12. How many hours does an event really take door to door, including setup, teardown, and anything afterward?

**The market**

13. Who's your competition locally and what do they charge? Rough is fine.
14. Anything you can do that they can't, or vice versa?
15. What kind of event do you want more of? Weddings and corporate parties are different businesses.
16. When someone books you instead of a competitor, do you know why?

**How people find you now**

17. Where does an inquiry come from today — referral, Instagram, The Knot, a venue, word of mouth from an event you MC'd?
18. Does the photobooth have its own name, logo, Instagram, or website, or is it "a thing Jeremy also does"?
19. Have you ever paid for advertising for it? What happened?

**You**

20. How many hours a week can you put into this between now and December?
21. What part of this do you actually hate doing? I'd rather automate what you dread than what's merely inefficient.
22. If this worked better than expected — say it's doing $75k a year in two years — is that something you'd want, or is the photobooth meant to stay a side business?
