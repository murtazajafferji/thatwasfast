Questions people always ask.

Will callers know it's AI?
Almost never. Modern voice AI (2026-era) is indistinguishable from a human in blind tests when tuned properly. Natural pauses, laughs, "uh-huh"s, handles interruptions. We legally disclose "AI assistant" only if a caller directly asks — most never do.
What if the AI can't answer a question?
Two paths: (1) polite escalation — "let me get someone on the line who can help with that" → warm transfer to your cell, or (2) capture — "let me have someone call you back within 30 min" → your team gets an urgent notification. Never a cold drop.
Do I need a new phone number?
No. We can attach the AI to your existing number in three ways: (1) forward missed/after-hours calls to it, (2) route it ahead of you and only ring your phone if the caller asks for a human, or (3) run it as your primary answering line. Your choice.

Any service business that lives and dies by inbound calls: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, med spas, dental, chiropractic, law firms, salons, event vendors, home services. If you're losing more than 5 calls a week, the math works out fast.

All calls are encrypted in transit and at rest. For medical/legal clients we run on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure (Vapi Enterprise or Retell with BAA). Recordings and transcripts stay in your account — we never train on your calls.

About $0.10-$0.20 per minute of actual conversation, depending on voice quality tier. A typical booked-appointment call is 2-4 minutes. If you handle 500 calls/month, expect ~$100-$200 in call costs on top of the $500/mo platform fee.
